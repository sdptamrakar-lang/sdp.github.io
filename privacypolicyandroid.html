Privacy Policy

_MAYA_

Effective Date: June 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how _MAYA_ (“we,” “our,” or “us”) handles user data and information related to our mobile games and applications. This policy applies directly to all applications and services published and distributed under the developer entity _MAYA_ on the Google Play Store.

We prioritize user privacy and do not directly collect personal identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, or phone number through our applications unless explicitly provided by you via direct support communications.

Information We Process

Our mobile applications integrate with trusted third-party service providers to optimize app performance and user experience. These services may automatically collect limited, non-sensitive diagnostic and usage identifiers, including:

  • Device Identifiers: Advertising identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID) where applicable by platform permissions.
  • Analytics & Usage Data: Interactions within the game, active sessions, and core gameplay engagement metrics.
  • Diagnostic Logs: Technical crash logs, system stability analysis, and overall application performance metrics.
  • Transaction Status: Non-financial validation statuses regarding whether an in-app purchase transaction was successfully completed.

Advertising and Monetization

To support our development, certain applications under this entity utilize third-party advertising networks to serve contextual or personalized advertisements. These networks handle data securely in accordance with their respective privacy standards.

In-App Purchases and Billing

Select applications may offer in-app purchases or subscriptions. All transactional processing is managed directly and securely by the Google Play Billing system.

We do not collect, process, or store financial billing credentials or credit card numbers. We only receive basic verification tokens from Google Play to validate and restore purchased digital content.

Purpose of Data Processing

Data retrieved via third-party SDKs is strictly limited to the following operational use cases:

  • Delivering core mobile app functionalities and gameplay features.
  • Serving advertisements to fund application accessibility.
  • Validating and restoring user-purchased entitlements.
  • Monitoring performance trends to resolve technical bugs and crash failures.
  • Protecting application infrastructure against fraudulent interactions.

Third-Party Integrations

Our applications securely interface with the following core systems:

  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK
  • Google Play Billing Services

Children’s Privacy Compliance

Our applications are structured to comply with global privacy standards and are not intentionally directed toward children under the age of 13 (or the legal threshold mapping to your local regional jurisdiction) unless specifically denoted otherwise on an individual application’s store metadata listing page.

User Controls & Choices

You maintain complete control over how advertising networks track data across your apps. You can opt out of personalized targeting parameters directly through your device settings (e.g., by navigating to Settings > Privacy > Ads on your Android operating system).

Security Measures

We employ standard digital safety measures to safeguard any operational data bound to our applications, though no transmission mechanism over the internet can be guaranteed as completely invulnerable.
