How can we help?

Find answers to common questions below or reach out to our development team directly.

I watched a rewarded ad but didn’t receive the reward
Sometimes rewards can be delayed if an ad network fails to confirm completion. Restart the game and check if the reward appears. If not, contact support with the game name, reward type, and approximate time it happened.
I was charged for an in-app purchase but didn’t receive it
First try using the "Restore Purchases" button in the game settings. If the item is still missing, contact support and include your purchase receipt or transaction ID if available.
How do I request a refund?
Refunds for purchases made through Apple are handled by Apple. You can request one through your Apple purchase history or Apple Support.
My progress was lost after reinstalling
Progress may only be recoverable if the game supports cloud saves, Game Center, or account syncing. Contact support and include details about the lost progress.
The app crashes or runs slowly
Make sure your device is updated and restart the app. If the problem continues, email support with your device model, OS version, and what happened before the issue occurred.
How can I remove personalized ads?
Ad preferences may be managed through your device privacy settings. Some apps may also offer an in-app option to remove ads.
How do I request deletion of my data?
If you have a privacy-related request, including deletion requests, contact support using the email below and include the app name in your message.
How do I restore my purchases?
Navigate to the settings menu inside the app and tap the "Restore Purchases" button. Ensure you are logged into the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.
I found a bug. How do I report it?
We appreciate your help! Please send an email to our support team using the button below. Include your device model, iOS version, and a brief description of the issue.
How do I manage my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed directly through Apple. Go to your iPhone Settings > Subscriptions to modify or cancel your plan.
